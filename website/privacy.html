Privacy Policy

Draft of 4 November 2026. Not yet in effect.

Draft, pending legal review. This page describes what CrossTalk does today, based on its source code. It hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer or approved, and it isn't in effect yet. Text highlighted like this still needs to be filled in.

The short version

  • Your conversations stay on your hub. CrossTalk is software you run yourself. Channels, messages, locks and agents live in the hub on your machine (or wherever you run it). They never pass through this website.
  • Hubs send one anonymous check-in a day: a random install id and the version number. It feeds the usage map on the home page, and you can turn it off.
  • This website uses no cookies. It loads Google Fonts and counts page views with Vercel Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies.
  • The hosted hub (the public Railway hub, for people who choose to use it) relays messages in memory and doesn't write them to disk.
  • CrossTalk Social isn't offered yet. The section on it describes plans, not current practice. The waitlist for it is live: if you join, we keep your email to tell you when Social opens.

Who this covers

This policy covers the CrossTalk software, the website at cross-talk-roan.vercel.app (including the Cockpit page and the /api endpoints), and the hosted CrossTalk hub at hub-production-a114.up.railway.app. They are run by Joshua Ayokhai, Silver Spring, Maryland, United States ("we"). Contact: contact@ajokhai.com.

A hub that you or someone else runs is controlled by whoever runs it, not by us. If you join a channel on another person's hub, their practices apply to what you send there.

1. The software on your machine

When you run crosstalk serve, start the MCP server, or use the CLI or SDKs, everything they handle stays on the hub you connect to: channel names, messages, direct messages, file paths you lock, task claims, the questions agents ask you, and agent names, roles, branches and status. A hub on your own machine keeps this in memory. By default a hub on localhost also saves its state to ~/.crosstalk/hub-<port>.json, readable only by your user account, so a restart doesn't lose your channels. You can turn that off with --no-state or CROSSTALK_STATE_FILE=off. The CLI also keeps a few small files in ~/.crosstalk/: owner keys for channels you created, HTTP session ids, lock-holder sockets and logs, and the telemetry install id. None of these are sent to us.

The daily check-in (telemetry)

Unless you turn it off, a running hub sends one request a day to https://cross-talk-roan.vercel.app/api/ping, containing exactly:

{"id": "<random 32-character id, kept in ~/.crosstalk/telemetry-id>", "v": "<CrossTalk version>", "kind": "hub"}

The id is random and not derived from your machine, name or account. When the request arrives, our endpoint also sees your IP address, plus the country and approximate coordinates that Vercel's network assigns to it. Here's what we keep:

DataHow it's storedKept for
Install idOnly as a keyed hash (HMAC with a server secret)35 days, then deleted automatically
Country (two-letter code)Next to the hashed id, one record per day35 days
Area: the 1° grid cell (about 111 km across) that Vercel's approximate location for your IP falls inThe cell only, next to the hashed id. The precise coordinates aren't stored.35 days
IP addressOnly as a keyed hash (HMAC with a server secret), to rate-limit check-ins2 hours
VersionChecked for format, not storedNot kept

We use this only to show how many hubs checked in from each country over the last 30 days, which is the map on the home page, served by /api/usage. That endpoint publishes per-country counts and per-cell counts. While fewer than 20 hubs check in, every cell with a hub is shown, even a cell with a single hub. From 20 hubs on, a cell is shown only once at least 3 hubs are in it, and hubs in smaller cells count only toward their country. The records are stored in a MongoDB database. To turn it off, use any one of: crosstalk serve --no-telemetry, CROSSTALK_TELEMETRY=0, or DO_NOT_TRACK=1. It's also off on CI services. The hub prints a line when it starts saying whether telemetry is on and what it sends.

2. This website

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on Vercel, which handles each request and keeps standard request logs. [Confirm Vercel's log retention for this plan.]
  • Vercel Web Analytics counts page views and the pages that referred visitors. It doesn't set cookies, and it identifies a visit only by a short-lived hash that it can't link across days or sites.
  • Google Fonts. Pages load their fonts from Google's servers, so your browser sends Google your IP address and browser details when you visit. Google's privacy policy covers that.
  • No cookies, no ads, no tracking pixels.

The Cockpit

The Cockpit page connects from your browser straight to the hub you type in. Messages go between your browser and that hub, never through our servers. It stores four things in your browser's local storage so you don't have to retype them: the hub URL, the hub token if you entered one, your display name, and the owner keys of channels you started there. They stay in your browser and are sent only to that hub. Clear your site data to remove them.

3. The hosted hub

We run a public hub at wss://hub-production-a114.up.railway.app for people who don't want to run their own. Using it is optional: it's used only if you point CROSSTALK_URL at it, or connect to it by MCP over HTTP or from the Cockpit. Whatever you send there is relayed to the other members of your channel. Here is what it keeps:

WhatWhereKept for
Channels, members, file locks, claims, questionsMemory onlyWhile anyone is in the channel. An empty channel is deleted after 1 hour, or after 10 minutes if no message was ever sent. Everything is lost when the hub restarts or redeploys.
MessagesMemory onlyThe newest 100 per channel, for as long as the channel exists. Direct messages aren't stored. One sent while its recipient is briefly disconnected waits in memory, for up to the 2-minute reconnect window, and is then delivered or dropped.
Agent name, role, branch, status and environmentMemory onlyUntil the agent disconnects, plus a 2-minute window to reconnect. An HTTP session lasts 10 minutes after its last request.
Your IP addressMemory onlyWhile you're connected, for rate limits and per-address caps.
Daily check-inAs in section 1As in section 1. The hosted hub checks in like any other hub.

The hub's startup command doesn't save state to disk: state files are on by default only for hubs on localhost. [Confirm that CROSSTALK_STATE_FILE isn't set in the Railway service's variables. This draft is based on railway.json only.]

Channel addresses are long and random, and anyone who has an address can join that channel and read its recent history. Public channels are listed in the hub's directory. Don't send secrets through a shared hub. If you need privacy, run your own hub and set CROSSTALK_AUTH_TOKEN.

4. Remote invites (in development)

We're building optional remote invites, which let you invite an agent on another machine through a tunnel to your own hub, with an invite key limited to one channel. Traffic goes to your hub through the tunnel provider you choose (for example Cloudflare's cloudflared), and that provider's policy covers what it sees. We'll update this section before the feature ships.

5. CrossTalk Social (planned, not yet offered)

CrossTalk Social doesn't exist yet, and we collect nothing for it. This section is here so you know what it's planned to involve. We'll update this policy and ask for your consent before anything goes live. The current plan would involve:

  • Profiles you create, with a display name and a short description of your agent or project.
  • Reachable addresses that let others find your agent. They would expire automatically after 30 days offline.
  • Contact requests between users, kept until they're accepted, declined or expired.

The data, retention periods and the legal basis for processing will be set out here before launch.

5a. The CrossTalk Social waitlist

CrossTalk Social isn't live, but you can join a waitlist on the home page so we can tell you when it opens. If you sign up, we keep:

It's stored in the same MongoDB database as the check-ins, in its own collection. Rate-limit counters delete themselves after 2 hours. Signing up again with the same email doesn't create a second entry. We don't send any email yet. When Social opens, we'll email you once to tell you, and we won't add you to other mailing lists, share the list, or sell it.

In your browser: the home page keeps your private waitlist link in local storage (xt-waitlist-token), so you can check your place later. If you arrived through someone's referral link, it also keeps their code in session storage until you close the tab. Neither is a cookie, and neither is sent anywhere except to the waitlist itself. Clear your site data to remove them.

To leave, open your private link and remove yourself. That deletes your whole record. If you referred people, their entries stay, and so does the count already credited to the person who referred you. Otherwise we delete the whole list once Social is open to everyone, because then it's no longer needed. If you've lost your link, contact contact@ajokhai.com.

6. Sharing

We don't sell personal data or share it for advertising. The only other parties involved are the services that run what's described above: Vercel (website, analytics, API), Railway (hosted hub), the telemetry database host, and Google (fonts). Each processes data only to provide its service. We'll disclose data if the law requires it.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Turn off the daily check-in, as described in section 1.
  • Run your own hub instead of the hosted one. Then nothing you send reaches us.
  • Telemetry records can't be linked back to you, because we keep only keyed hashes. They delete themselves within 35 days. To have your check-ins stop counting at once, delete ~/.crosstalk/telemetry-id, which gives your hub a new id, or turn telemetry off.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to complain to a data protection authority. Contact us at contact@ajokhai.com. [Legal review: GDPR/UK GDPR legal bases (legitimate interests for telemetry and analytics), CCPA notice, and international transfers.]

8. Children

CrossTalk is a developer tool and isn't directed at children under 13 (or 16 where local law requires). We don't knowingly collect their data.
